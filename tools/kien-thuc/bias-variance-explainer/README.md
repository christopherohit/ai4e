# bias-variance-explainer

Animated explainer of the bias–variance tradeoff on real data: Auto MPG (mpg from horsepower). Four chapters:
underfitting and overfitting on 30 cars, many random training sets with a dartboard of predictions, the error
decomposition, and the effect of more data. Article content stays in blog/src/content/posts/.

- Tool.astro: entry imported by the blog adapter (chrome from tools/_shared/glass-lab/).
- model.ts: seeded subsets, Legendre-basis least squares, the local-linear reference curve and the decomposition.
- render.ts: the four animated scenes.
- Data: tools/_shared/glass-lab/data/auto-mpg.json, generated by scripts/ml-datasets/prepare.py.
