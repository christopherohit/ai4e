# svm-explainer

Animated explainer of support vector machines on the Iris flowers: sweeping candidate lines, the maximum-margin street
and its support vectors (click to remove flowers), the soft margin and C, and the RBF kernel as a lifted surface.
Article content stays in blog/src/content/posts/.

- Tool.astro: entry imported by the blog adapter (chrome from tools/_shared/glass-lab/).
- model.ts: the three Iris problems and an SMO solver with an error cache, linear and RBF kernels, cross-validation.
- render.ts: the four animated scenes.
- data/iris.json: generated by scripts/ml-datasets/prepare.py, with scikit-learn reference results.
