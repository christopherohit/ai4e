# regularization-explainer

Animated explainer comparing L1 (Lasso) and L2 (Ridge) on real data: the Diabetes dataset of Efron et al. (2004).
Four chapters: the diamond and circle on a two-feature loss (BMI, blood pressure), shrink vs soft-threshold, the Lasso
and Ridge coefficient paths with 10-fold cross-validation, and bootstrap instability of two correlated cholesterol
measures. Article content stays in blog/src/content/posts/.

- Tool.astro: entry imported by the blog adapter (chrome from tools/_shared/glass-lab/).
- model.ts: standardization, closed-form Ridge, coordinate-descent Lasso, paths, cross-validation and bootstrap.
- render.ts: the four animated scenes.
- data/diabetes.json: generated by scripts/ml-datasets/prepare.py, with the scikit-learn Lasso path as reference.
